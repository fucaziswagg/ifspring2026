🦋 Help Protect Monarch Butterflies — They Need Us Now

Monarch butterflies are one of nature's greatest travelers, completing a journey of nearly 3,000 miles even though each one weighs less than a dime. But this incredible migration is now in serious danger. In the last twenty years, monarch populations have dropped by more than 90%, and without quick action, their yearly journey could disappear within our lifetime.
The biggest reason for this decline is the loss of habitat. Monarchs need native milkweed to lay their eggs and nectar plants to fuel their long flights. As development, pesticides, and the removal of overwintering trees continue, the places monarchs depend on are vanishing fast.
